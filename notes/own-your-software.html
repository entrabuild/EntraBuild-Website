Why a pharma company chose to own its sales software

Renting software is quick to start. Owning it pays off once your process is your advantage.

Zivira Labs had used a rented sales app for years. It worked, but every change they wanted waited on someone else's release plan, and their data sat on someone else's servers.

We started with the daily call report, the screen every medical rep fills in after each doctor visit. After the first demo, their team asked for more detail, and the form grew from 3 fields to 9 within a week. With rented software that request would have gone on a wish list.

Approvals now flow from rep to manager to head office, with a 24-hour window before reports reach admin, and a separate HR portal handles attendance. Zivira owns the code and pays its own hosting directly.

The work is planned in phases, so each phase is paid for only after the one before it is in use.
