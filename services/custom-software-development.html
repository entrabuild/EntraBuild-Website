Custom software built around how your team works

Portals for every role, approvals, reports and mobile apps. We replace spreadsheets, paper books and rented tools with software you own.

Get a quoteSee packages

Off-the-shelf tools make your team change how it works. We do the opposite: we study how work actually moves through your company, then build the software around it, with a separate portal for each role from field staff to head office.

We have replaced a pharma company's rented sales tool with its own platform, turned a construction company's handwritten books into a billing and stock system, and built multi-portal platforms for speech data teams. Projects run in phases, so you start using the software early and pay as each phase is delivered.

What you get

  • Separate portals for each role (staff, managers, admin, super admin)
  • Approval flows, reports and Excel exports
  • Android and iOS apps when your team works in the field
  • Cloud hosting on accounts in your name
  • Code and data are yours after each phase is paid
  • AI features added in later phases

Questions

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the number of roles, screens and integrations. We start with a requirements document and a phased proposal, so you know the cost of each phase before it starts.

Can you replace software we pay for every month?

Often, yes. We map every screen you use today and rebuild what you need, so you stop paying per user and own the result.

Who hosts the software?

It runs on cloud platforms such as Render, Vercel, Cloudflare or Google Cloud, preferably on accounts in your company's name.
