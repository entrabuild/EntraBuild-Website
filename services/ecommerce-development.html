Questions

How much does an online store cost?

Online stores are quoted after a short call, because the price depends on the number of products, payment methods and extras like a chatbot. You always get a fixed quote before we start.

Can customers order on WhatsApp instead of paying online?

Yes. Many of our clients prefer WhatsApp ordering. The store sends the full order to your WhatsApp and you confirm payment directly.

Can I add products myself?

Yes. You get an admin dashboard where you can add products, change prices and see orders from your phone or computer.
