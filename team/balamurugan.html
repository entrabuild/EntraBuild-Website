About Balamurugan

I am Balamurugan, an AI Developer at Entraiot Solutions. I completed my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Karpagam Institute of Technology. I am passionate about developing innovative AI-powered applications and exploring emerging technologies.

At EntraBuild, I have been involved in developing Entra Connect, an integrated platform with multiple dashboards and management portals. My work includes building interactive user interfaces, developing 3D animated experiences, integrating AI-powered features, and contributing to the overall platform development.

My technical skills include Python, React.js, JavaScript, AI application development, and UI/UX design. I also work with AI development tools such as Claude and Blender to create interactive 3D experiences and modern web applications.

I enjoy learning new technologies, solving real-world problems, and transforming creative ideas into practical digital solutions. My goal is to continuously improve my technical skills and contribute to building innovative, user-friendly applications.
